Friendly, force-free corgi training — one easy step at a time.

What we make

CorgiLogix publishes short, illustrated, step-by-step videos that show corgi owners exactly what to do: potty training, sit, stay, loose-leash walking, gentle fixes for herding nips, and more. Every lesson uses positive reinforcement only.

Publishing to YouTube

The CorgiLogix app is a private tool used only by the channel owner to produce these videos and publish them to the CorgiLogix YouTube channel using the YouTube API Services. When connected to YouTube, it:

  • uploads videos the owner created to the owner's own channel,
  • sets each video's title, description, thumbnail, captions and release time,
  • checks whether a scheduled video has gone live.

It does not access anyone else's account and does not sell or share any data. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Posting to TikTok

The app also posts the owner's videos to the CorgiLogix TikTok account with TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. Before each post, the owner sees the connected account, reviews the caption, and chooses who can view it and whether comments, Duets and Stitches are allowed. Nothing is posted without that review.
