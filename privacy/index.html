Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the CorgiLogix app ("the app") handles information. The app is a private tool used by the owner of the CorgiLogix YouTube channel to create corgi training videos and publish them to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services to publish videos to YouTube. By using the app's YouTube features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Information the app accesses

When the channel owner connects the app to YouTube and grants permission, the app uses the YouTube API Services only to:

  • upload videos the owner created to the owner's own YouTube channel;
  • set those videos' titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, privacy status and scheduled release time;
  • read the status of those videos (for example, whether a scheduled video has gone live);
  • read the connected channel's name, to show which channel is connected.

The app does not access, collect or store viewers' data, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any information from any other YouTube account.

TikTok

The app also uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API so the channel owner can post their own videos to their own CorgiLogix TikTok account. With the owner's permission, it uses them only to:

  • read the connected account's basic profile (display name and avatar), to show which account is connected and to display it on the posting screen as TikTok requires;
  • read the account's posting options (such as available privacy settings and whether comments, Duets and Stitches are allowed), so the owner can choose them before each post;
  • upload and publish videos the owner created, with the caption, privacy and interaction settings the owner chose, and check each post's publishing status.

The app does not access followers, likes, comments, messages, analytics or any other account's information. By using the app's TikTok features you agree to TikTok's Terms of Service; TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to publish and schedule the owner's own videos, and to show the owner whether a video has been published. The app does not sell, rent or share any information with third parties, does not use it for advertising, and sends nothing to any server other than Google's when publishing to YouTube.

Storage, cookies and device data

  • The app runs only on the channel owner's own computer. The sign-in (OAuth) tokens for YouTube and TikTok, the IDs of videos the app uploaded, and their last-checked status are stored there, in local files. Nothing is stored on external servers.
  • The app doesn't use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics or advertising identifiers.
  • This website doesn't use cookies or tracking.

Data retention and deletion

  • Information obtained from the YouTube API Services (video IDs and release status) is kept only while it's needed to manage a scheduled video. It is refreshed whenever the app checks a video, and it's deleted or refreshed within 30 days, as the YouTube API Services Developer Policies require.
  • Deleting a scheduled post in the app deletes its stored record.
  • Clicking Disconnect in the app deletes the stored sign-in token from the computer immediately.
  • To have all app data deleted, disconnect the app and revoke its access as described below. Any remaining local files are deleted when the app is uninstalled. You can also contact us (see below) to ask that any data be deleted.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time from Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. You can remove its access to your TikTok account from the app permissions section of the TikTok app's settings (Settings and privacy → Security & permissions). You can also use Disconnect in the app for either platform, which deletes that platform's stored tokens from the computer immediately.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact us through the CorgiLogix YouTube channel.
