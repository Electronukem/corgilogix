Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the CorgiLogix app ("the app") handles information. The app is a private tool used by the owner of the CorgiLogix YouTube channel to create corgi training videos and publish them to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

Information the app accesses

When the channel owner connects the app to YouTube, the app uses the YouTube API Services, with the owner's permission, only to:

  • upload videos the owner created to the owner's own YouTube channel;
  • set those videos' titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, privacy status and scheduled release time;
  • read the status of those videos (for example, whether a scheduled video has gone live).

The app does not read, collect or store viewers' data, comments, subscribers or any other account's information.

How information is stored

The sign-in token that lets the app upload is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Videos, scripts and schedules are also stored only on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's when publishing to YouTube.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any information with third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using the app's YouTube features you are also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the app ("Disconnect") or from your Google Account's third-party access settings. Disconnecting deletes the stored sign-in token from the computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
